# Ultimate Learn

A free learning app that runs in the browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

**Play it:** https://andreasgraykristoffersen.github.io/ultimate-learn/

## What's inside

- **Piano**: 28 songs to learn by pressing the gold keys. Record yourself and play it back. Turn on the microphone and it listens to your real piano.
- **Sing**: sing along with gold note bars while a line shows your voice. Earn up to 3 stars per song.
- **Draw**: free drawing, or step-by-step lessons (heart, sun, house, cat, fish, flower, car).
- **Guitar**: tap chords and swipe to strum, learn chord songs, and tune a real guitar with the microphone.

Every page has a Back button, so it's easy to find your way around.

All songs are old public-domain tunes. Everything is in a single `index.html` file.
